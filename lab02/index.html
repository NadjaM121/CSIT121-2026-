Labs 1 & 2

This is my first webpage, and at first I was really confused because there were so many different things I had to learn. I am still trying to understand how all the different parts of HTML work together, but it gets easier when I can actually see what I am doing on the webpage. I worked on the layout one piece at a time and used the HTML Notes page in the online textbook when I got stuck or forgot how to use a tag. Looking at my code line by line helped me figure out what was causing problems and understand what I was doing. Web development is still challenging for me, but being able to see the changes I make on the webpage helps me understand it better.

What I Learned

  1. I learned how to put everything together on my own from the beginning. At first it was confusing, but once I started doing it myself, I started to get the hang of it.
  2. I learned how to figure things out without having an exact example to copy from the professor. I had to really try things on my own and figure out what worked.
  3. I learned that I actually enjoy making webpages once I get the hang of it. It made me feel proud because I felt like I actually created something on my own. I know I might not remember everything right away, but when I am actually working on it, it starts to make more sense.

Learning about HTML and web standards was another interesting part of this assignment. I learned a lot from the online textbook and the HTML Notes page because they helped me understand what I was doing and how the different parts of HTML work together. Honestly, I don't think I would have understood half of what I was doing without using the textbook and going back to the notes when I got stuck.
